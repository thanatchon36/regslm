# Disclaimer

`regslm` is an **independent open-source project** by Thanatchon Chongmankhong.

- It uses **public documents only**: rulebooks, regulations and guidelines published on
  regulators' public websites or official legal portals. Every text traces to a URL and
  SHA-256 in `data/corpus/manifest.parquet`.
- It does **not** represent the views, policies or supervisory practice of any institution,
  including the author's employer. No non-public material was used.
- Thai legal texts are included as one language among several; nothing in this project
  comments on any Thai institution's policy.
- Nothing here is legal, regulatory or compliance advice. Model output is a machine
  extraction that can be wrong, incomplete or out of date, and **must be reviewed by a
  qualified person before any use**.
